# Budget-OX: UI/UX audit (Oct 21, 2026)

**Method:** design-critique and accessibility-review (WCAG 2.1 AA) checklists from Anthropic's Design plugin. I ran the web build (`localhost:8081`) at 390×844 (iPhone size) and measured contrast and tap-target sizes in the page. **Screens covered:** Accueil, Activité, Budget, Agenda, Wallet. I didn't open the detail screens. The web build may differ slightly from Expo Go on Android.

## Overall impression
It's a polished, consistent dark UI with a clear "money first" hierarchy, and the screen-reader labels are excellent. The biggest problem is **trust in the numbers**: the same concept shows different values on different tabs. After that come grey secondary text that's too faint, and a few spacing and overlap glitches.

## 🔴 Critical

| # | Finding | Where | Fix |
|---|---------|-------|-----|
| 1 | **Three different "spending this month" totals.** Accueil shows Dépenses −1 440,74$, Budget shows Dépensé 2 410$, and Agenda shows Sorties −2 166,60$. A budget app loses credibility when its totals disagree. | Accueil, Budget, Agenda | Use one source of truth for "spent this month". If the numbers really measure different things, say so in the label ("Dépenses (transactions)" / "Engagé (budgets)" / "Paiements récurrents"). |
| 2 | **Net worth differs between tabs.** Accueil shows "Patrimoine net 14 481,60$" and Wallet shows "Valeur nette 7 418,79$". | 3 | **Nested buttons in the Wallet.** The "Afficher les enveloppes" chevron is a Pressable inside the account row's Pressable. On web this is invalid HTML (React raises a hydration error). On native, the two tap areas compete, and screen readers announce both. | `components/wallet/ProtoWalletHub.tsx:440` | Make the chevron a sibling of the row's pressable rather than a child, or have the row do one thing (expand) and put "open account" elsewhere. Also check `components/kit/motion.tsx:36`, which wraps a Pressable around children that already contain a Pressable. |

## 🟡 Moderate

| # | Finding | Where | Fix |
|---|---------|-------|-----|
| 4 | **Secondary grey text fails contrast.** It measures 3.0–4.1:1 against the 4.5:1 required. This affects "Bonjour, Jérémie", Revenus/Dépenses/Épargne labels, category subtitles ("Épicerie", "Voyages"), date headers (AUJ., HIER), "8 catégories sur 9…", and "+4 conseils" (3.02:1, the worst). | All tabs | Lighten the muted text token by about 15 %. | 5 | **Text too small.** Bottom-tab labels are **9px** (`BottomNavBar.tsx:220`), many labels are 11px, and there are about 40 text nodes under 12px per screen. | Tab bar, section headers | Make tab labels at least 11px, and body or secondary text at least 12–13px. Respect the system font scale. |

| 7 | **Floating buttons cover content.** The green "+" and the voice FAB hide the Petro-Canada amount on Activité. The white "+" on Agenda hides the iCloud+ amount. | Activité, Agenda | Add bottom padding to the list (FAB height + 16) and/or hide the FAB while the user scrolls down. |
| 8 | **Header row overflows on Activité.** The search field and two icon buttons don't fit at 390pt; the calendar button and the "Revenus" segment are cut off on the right. | Activité | Use `flex:1` on search with fixed 44pt icon buttons, and size the segmented control to the screen width. |
| 9 | **Progress rings stay green when nearly full.** Logement at 100 % and Restaurants at 79 % have green rings even though the "À surveiller" list flags them. | Budget grid, Accueil | Use green below about 75 %, amber from about 75 to 100 %, and red above 100 %. | 10 | **Tab name ≠ screen title.** The tab is called "ACTIVITÉ" but the screen is titled "Transactions". | 11 | **Budget screen is blank for about 2s** on first open before content fades in. | Budget | Shorten or remove the entry fade on tab screens, or show a skeleton. |

## Accessibility summary (WCAG 2.1 AA)
- **1.4.3 Contrast:** ❌ secondary text at 3.0–4.1:1 (items 4 and 13).
- **2.5.5 Target size:** ❌ many targets at 28–43pt (item 6).
- **4.1.2 Name/role/value:** ✅ every button has a descriptive label (for example "Prochain paiement : Disney+, 12,99$, Demain"), and tabs use `role=tab`. ❌ nested buttons in the Wallet (item 3).
- **Text size / scaling:** ⚠️ 9–11px labels (item 5). Verify with Android font scale set to 1.3×.

## What works well
- Each screen has a strong hierarchy: a large bold amount, then a supporting label, then detail.
- Cards, radii, icon wells and spacing are consistent; it feels like one design system.
- The Copilote card is a good pattern: a short count ("3 points à regarder") plus the top two insights.
- The "À surveiller" section on Budget, with "Projection fin du mois", is the most useful thing in the app.
- Screen-reader labels are unusually thorough.

## Top 3 priorities
1. **Reconcile the totals** (items 1 and 2). This is the single biggest trust issue.
2. **Raise the muted-text contrast and the minimum font size** (items 4 and 5). It's one token change that fixes most screens.
3. **Fix the Wallet's nested buttons, FAB overlap and header overflow** (items 3, 7 and 8). These are small code fixes for visible glitches.
